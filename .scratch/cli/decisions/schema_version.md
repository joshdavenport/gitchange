The envelope carries an integer `schema_version` — a field, not a flag: a `--porcelain=v2`-style flag would freeze v1 on day one, and gitchange has no installed base to protect. The version is global to the JSON dialect: `status --json` and `diff --json` share types (change kinds, stage tokens, the advisory shape), so a breaking change to either bumps the one integer for both; at build time one wire module owns the constant and the serialisers, so the two surfaces cannot drift. The contract published beside it: adding a field, adding a member to a string-typed enum, and populating a previously-null field are not breaking and do not bump the version; renaming, removing, retyping, or changing the meaning of a field does. Consumers must ignore unknown fields and unknown enum values. Distinct from the state-file version, whose mismatch is a refusal — two versions, two policies, easy to confuse.
