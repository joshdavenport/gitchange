Any surface that speaks before a claim is made — the capture-pending hint, the records-guard refusal, the forced-delete notice — names the mechanism (recordless hunks are claimed at the next persisting refresh) and never a landing spot. The destination is context-derived twice over: an intervening `switch` moves it, and the entry-unit rule (#106, `matcher.rs`) overrides it hunk-by-hunk — a recordless hunk in an index entry with an established owner ≠ active joins that owner, not the active changelist. An accurate forecast would preview context-derived ownership on a read path, which the reads-never-persist decision forbids, so a named destination is either wrong or forbidden. The claiming refresh's receipt (`AutoCaptured`, `EntryUnitCapture`) reports where hunks actually landed, once, to the actor who triggered it.
